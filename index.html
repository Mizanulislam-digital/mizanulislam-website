Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about services, pricing, and how I work

What services does Mizanul Islam offer?

Mizanul Islam offers Meta Ads management, Google Ads campaigns, Meta CAPI tracking setup, conversion rate optimization (CRO), and WhatsApp Cloud API automation. Currently open to new client collaborations — based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, working with clients globally.

How much does a discovery call cost?

The 15-minute discovery call is free. Intro strategy calls (45 minutes) are $49, and full system audits (90 minutes) are $149. Monthly retainers are custom-quoted after the audit based on scope and channels.

How long does a Meta CAPI tracking setup take?

A standard Meta CAPI + server-side tracking setup takes 5-10 business days. Meta Ads management runs monthly with weekly optimization. Funnel and CRO projects typically take 2-4 weeks depending on scope.

Does Mizanul Islam work with international clients?

Yes. Mizanul works with clients globally, including Bangladesh, UAE, and other markets. All communication is in English or Bengali.

What tools and platforms does Mizanul Islam use?

Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, GA4, Google Tag Manager, Meta CAPI, Cloudflare Zaraz, Looker Studio, WhatsApp Cloud API, Microsoft Clarity, and custom tracking infrastructure built on Cloudflare Pages.
