Data Protection & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Transparent guidelines explaining how Grow Up collects, processes, and safeguards your personal data across our software solutions, tracking pipelines, and strategy services.

Effective Date: August 22, 2026
Operated By: Grow Up (Mizanul Islam)

1. Introduction

At Mizanul Islam and parent organization Grow Up ("Company", "we", "us", "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle user information collected when you visit our website, use our marketing tools (UTM Builder, WA Link Generator), submit application forms, or interact with our server-side tracking setups.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to deliver effective performance marketing solutions, process strategy bookings, and optimize system functionality:

  • Directly Provided Information: Full name, professional email address, phone/WhatsApp number, portfolio links, and monthly ad budget scope submitted through contact, job application, or booking forms.
  • Automated Usage Data: IP address, browser user-agent, operating system details, referring URLs, and interaction logs captured via standard web analytics and diagnostic tools.
  • First-Party Conversion & Event Data: Event parameters (e.g., pageviews, form clicks) captured through server-side containers for attribution accuracy.
Notice on Server-Side Tracking & Meta CAPI: We implement Server-Side Google Tag Manager (sGTM) and Meta Conversions API (CAPI) infrastructure. All customer parameters transmitted to ad networks are hashed using secure SHA-256 protocols before transmission to ensure privacy-compliant, first-party data processing.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly utilized for operational, analytical, and communication purposes, including:

  • Processing job applications and career candidate profiles for Grow Up roles.
  • Scheduling 1-on-1 strategy consultation calls and transmitting invoice confirmations.
  • Delivering automated WhatsApp notifications when opted-in.
  • Improving overall site performance, user interface layout, and tool reliability.
  • Ensuring website security and preventing automated abuse or spam submissions.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party marketers. Data is shared exclusively with verified service infrastructure providers under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Cloud & Analytics Infrastructure: Cloudflare Workers, Google Analytics 4 (Server-Stream), and Vercel/Netlify hosting environments.
  • Communication APIs: Meta WhatsApp Cloud API and transactional email service pipelines.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by lawful judicial orders or regulatory mandates in accordance with Bangladesh law.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We utilize essential first-party cookies and local storage items to maintain session states, navigation preferences, and security checks. Users may disable cookies through browser settings, though certain interactive marketing tools may operate with limited functionality as a result.

6. Data Retention & Security Standards

We enforce robust technical safeguards—including SSL encryption, Content Security Policy (CSP) headers, and strict access controls—to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Form submissions and application details are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the requested service or recruitment cycle.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we store about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your submitted contact details.
  • Opt-out of automated marketing messages or WhatsApp broadcast lists at any time.

8. Third-Party Web Links

Our website contains relative links to external tools, case studies, and partner resources. Grow Up is not responsible for the privacy practices or content hosted on external third-party domains.

9. Updates to Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changing legal requirements or system infrastructure upgrades. Revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
